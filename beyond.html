Beyond research

Away from academia

Between research positions I worked outside the university. I co-founded a small company, and spent time in places built around community and sustainability. I like working with my hands to build things and keeping my body moving, and these years gave me plenty of both: I ran daily operations for an organic farming collective, lived as a community member at an earth-building centre, and volunteered on food-forest and vernacular-architecture projects.

Looking back, that time away from academia has been the most valuable time I have had. It opened me up to life, and to ways of thinking, that I would never have found from inside a university, and it gave me room to explore mindfulness practices and alternative spaces. I keep up a daily mindfulness practice, with occasional residential Vipassana and Satipaṭṭhāna courses. If you ever get the chance to step outside academia for a while, I would recommend it to anyone.
