Beyond research

Away from academia

Between research positions I have worked outside the university: I co-founded a small company, and spent time in places built around community, sustainability and meditation. Time away from academia turned out to be a good chance to explore mindfulness practices and alternative spaces. I like working with my hands to build things and keeping my body moving, and these years gave me plenty of both. I have run daily operations for an organic farming collective, lived as a community member at an earth-building centre, and volunteered on food-forest and vernacular-architecture projects. I also keep up a daily mindfulness practice, with occasional residential Vipassana and Satipaṭṭhāna courses.

  • 2026
    Dhaanyam Food Forest, Attappadi — volunteer, working on tribal architecture and sustainability.
  • 2025
    Urvi Earth Centre, Munnar — community member; participated in community and meditation activities, and built mud houses using local, earthen materials.
  • 2024
    Farmer's Share Permaculture and Craft Learning Center, Shornur — ran daily operations for an organic farming collective and led immersive educational camps on sustainable living and permaculture.
  • 2022 – 2024
    Growers Haven LLP, Pollachi — founding partner; ran operations end to end, managed people and suppliers, and delivered against deadlines.

Outside work

Away from the desk I love music, finding new spots to swim and hike, losing evenings to jigsaw puzzles, playing chess, and trying new food wherever I travel. Every now and then I like to just sit in silence.
